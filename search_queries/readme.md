# Search Query Datasets

This folder contains datasets used to search and analyze the SwissAI filtered training data for potentially harmful or offensive information.

## Motivation

Our main objective is to explore the presence and distribution of potentially harmful or offensive content within the SwissAI filtered training data pipeline.

## Datasets

* **WeaponizedWords**: Access restricted to research teams upon special request. We cannot disclose this dataset as per the Terms of Service at weaponizedword.org, which prevents unauthorized sharing of proprietary data.

* **Obscene Words**: A list of profane and offensive terms. Available at: https://github.com/LDNOOBW/List-of-Dirty-Naughty-Obscene-and-Otherwise-Bad-Words

* **Chemical Dataset**: List of dangerous chemicals selected from "A Laboratory History Of Chemical Warfare Agents" by Jared Ledgard (2006).

* **Misinformation Keywords**: A ChatGPT-generated list of keywords associated with common misinformation topics.
